Level 4 - Bridging Grade span: K
1.

Enrich sentences with vivid verbs and adjectives (integrated ELD) — During a shared writing session under a document camera, return to the student's own line "then thatha fell ofe the stoole" and ask: "What kind of stool was it? How did Thatha fall — did he tumble, crash, slip?" Scribe the student's richer wording (e.g., "Suddenly, Thatha slipped off the tall stool") and add it to the class chart of vivid words, just as the class replaces plain verbs like "knocked" with "destroyed" or "demolished" during story reconstruction. Ch. 3 • p. 192–194

2.

Use a story-stage template to expand the narrative (integrated ELD) — Provide a 'Story Rewriting Template' with the three labeled stages the student already knows — orientation, complication, resolution— and invite the student to add one more sentence to each section, using the sentence frame "Suddenly, ___ because ___" for the complication and "In the end, ___ felt ___ because ___" for the resolution, then share the expanded version with a partner and add details based on their questions.
